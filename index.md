# Privacy Policy for Hand Hatch

**Last Updated**: February 22, 2026

## Introduction

Hand Hatch ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

**We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.**

Hand Hatch is designed with privacy in mind. All your data stays on your device:

- Exercise selections and preferences
- Progress and completion history
- XP and achievement data
- Session history
- Difficulty settings
- Egg hatching and creature collection progress
- Pain check-in responses
- User display name (optional, entered during onboarding)

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using:
- AsyncStorage (local device storage)
- No cloud synchronization
- No external databases
- No third-party analytics services

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Share your data with third parties
- Sell your information
- Track your activity outside the app
- Use advertising networks
- Collect analytics or crash reports

## Data Security

Your data is protected by:
- Your device's security features
- Local storage only (no internet transmission)
- No account creation required
- No authentication servers

## Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by:
- Uninstalling the app (removes all data permanently)
- Clearing app data in your device settings

## Children's Privacy

Hand Hatch does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we don't collect any data at all, the app is safe for all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
